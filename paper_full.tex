\begin{abstract}
We propose a CG-corrected a posteriori residual certificate for the discrete elliptic system $A_h u_h = f$. Starting from the standard bound $\|u_h-\hat{u}\|_2 \le \frac{\|r\|_2}{\lambda_{\min}(A_h)}$, we compute $z \approx A_h^{-1}r$ via CG and form $B_{CG} = \|z\|_2 + \frac{\|r-A_h z\|_2}{\alpha}$ with $\alpha \le \lambda_{\min}(A_h)$. Across coefficient contrasts, ID/OOD, and real FNO predictions, $B_{CG}$ is near-sharp (effectivity $\approx 1.0$) while preserving coverage 1.0 and FNR 0.0. The certificate enables selective verification by accepting predictions when $B_{CG} \le \tau$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Error certification for learned PDE surrogates is needed to decide when a prediction is trustworthy. For SPD elliptic systems, residual-based bounds provide safety but can be overly conservative with global coercivity constants.

\section{Theory}
Let $A_h \succ 0$, $u_h=A_h^{-1}f$, $r=f-A_h\hat{u}$, $e=u_h-\hat{u}=A_h^{-1}r$. For any $z$, $e=z+A_h^{-1}(r-A_h z)$. Thus
$$
\|e\|_2 \le \|z\|_2 + \frac{\|r-A_h z\|_2}{\lambda_{\min}(A_h)} \le \|z\|_2 + \frac{\|r-A_h z\|_2}{\alpha},\ \alpha\le\lambda_{\min}(A_h).
$$
We take $z$ as CG approximation of $A_h^{-1}r$.

\section{Assumptions}
(1) Discrete SPD system from coercive $-\nabla\cdot(a\nabla u)=f$, $a\ge a_{\min}>0$. (2) $\alpha=a_{\min}\lambda_{\min}(-\Delta_h)\le\lambda_{\min}(A_h)$. (3) Double-precision arithmetic; CG stopping controls $\|r-A_h z\|$. (4) Certification is w.r.t. discrete $u_h$.
