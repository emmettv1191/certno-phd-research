\begin{abstract}
We develop an a posteriori error certification framework for learned surrogates of elliptic PDEs. For the discrete SPD system $A_h u_h = f$, any candidate $\hat{u}$ satisfies $\|u_h - \hat{u}\|_2 \le \|r\|_2 / \lambda_{\min}(A_h)$ with $r=f-A_h\hat{u}$. We use a closed-form coercivity bound on $\lambda_{\min}(A_h)$ to obtain a cheap, computable certificate with explicit validity assumptions ($a\ge a_{\min}$). A selective verification policy abstains and falls back to the reference solver only when the bound exceeds tolerance or hypotheses are violated. We evaluate coverage, sharpness, and false-negative rate under controlled distribution shift, and demonstrate the approach on 1D elliptic problems with FNO/DeepONet baselines. We also analyze identifiability limits and goal-oriented active learning for quantities of interest.
\end{abstract}

\section{Theory}
For $-\nabla\cdot(a\nabla u)=f$ on $(0,1)^d$ with Dirichlet data and $a\ge a_{\min}>0$, the discrete operator $A_h\succ 0$ yields
$$
\|u_h - \hat{u}\|_2 \le \frac{\|f - A_h \hat{u}\|_2}{\lambda_{\min}(A_h)} \le \frac{\|f - A_h \hat{u}\|_2}{a_{\min}\,\lambda_{\min}(-\Delta_h)}.
$$
Validity requires $a_{\min}^{(\text{ass})}\le \min_i a_i$. When violated, we do not claim the bound and trigger verification.

\section{Experiments}
Empirical studies show high coverage under moderate OOD shifts with conservative (loose) bounds; looseness increases with coefficient contrast (analytic vs discrete-exact ratio grows with contrast). Selective verification saves solver calls when tolerance is permissive relative to error distributions.

\section{Limitations}
The bound is for the discrete system; anisotropy/tightness and extension to H^1/energy norms remain open. Residual-based diagnostics cannot resolve the scaling $(a,f)\equiv(ca,cf)$ without additional priors.
